Our commercial proposals have a few embarrassing details, and clients have started to notice.

- The header says "Freight quote to <client>" - it should read "for <client>".
- Quantities are wrong for single items: "1 pallets", and "3 boxs" instead of "3 boxes". One item is singular, several are plural, and the usual English endings apply.
- Amounts are hard to read: 12345.67 should be shown with thousands separators and always two decimals ($12,345.67, $0.05). Refunds currently come out as "$-3.50" instead of "-$3.50".

Please fix it everywhere the proposal text is produced. Don't change how totals or tax are calculated, and don't touch the database.
