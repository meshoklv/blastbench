When we pay a supplier once for several shipments, the whole payment is counted against each of those shipments. Pay $1,000.00 for three shipments and every one of them shows $1,000.00 of cost, so reports claim we spent $3,000.00 and margins look terrible.

What we want: each shipment carries only its own part of the payment, the parts add up to the payment exactly (not one cent lost or invented, so please think about amounts that don't divide evenly), and a payment for a single shipment still counts in full.

We already have real data with multi-shipment payments in it. After the change those old payments must come out right as well, and the change to the stored data has to be something we can roll back if the release goes wrong.
